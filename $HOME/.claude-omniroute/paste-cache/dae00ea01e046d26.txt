============================================================
6. PRICE SNAPSHOT — CRITICAL
============================================================

Think carefully about historical billing.

Example:

Today:

PAN Card = ₹250

Customer application billed:

₹250

Later:

PAN Card service price changes to:

₹300

The old invoice must remain:

₹250

Therefore:

billing must store a historical price snapshot.

Do NOT calculate old invoices dynamically from the current Service.base_price.

Recommended conceptual field:

service_amount

or:

unit_price

This is the price used at the time of billing.

The exact schema should follow the project's design.

============================================================
7. BILLING LINE ITEMS
============================================================

Design billing so it can represent:

PAN Card       ₹250

Discount       -₹30
--------------------
Final          ₹240

The base Service is the main service.

Additional charges must NOT alter the Service's permanent price.

Consider whether line items should be represented with:

invoice_items

or a JSON structure.

Prefer a normalized relational design if the project architecture
supports it and future reporting requires it.

Do not over-engineer.
